Run the rooftops

Your squirrel runs on its own across roofs, trees and power lines. Tap to jump, hold for a higher jump, and tap again in the air for a second hop. Fall between the roofs and the run is over.

Best played with your phone turned sideways.
2 / 3

Shema is listening

Every landing makes some noise. Tin roofs and big drops go over the red line, and crashing into a cat or a hadeda is the loudest of all. Over the line, Shema's beam swings to you and a red ring fills. If it closes, you're caught.

3 / 3

Hide in the noise

Jump over cats and hadedas. Leafy trees hide you from the beam, and rain or a passing taxi makes the street so noisy that Shema can hardly pick you out. To drop to a lower roof, just run off the edge. Grab acorns for points and the paw badge for 6 quiet seconds.
